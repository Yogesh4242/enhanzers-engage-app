# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026

Welcome to **Engage** ("we," "our," or "us"), a service operated by **Enhanzers**. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website and services at **https://engage.enhanzers.com**.

Depending on how you use Engage, we may collect the following information:

### Account Information

* Name
* Email address
* Business name
* Phone number
* Login credentials (encrypted where applicable)

### Business Information

* Restaurant or business details
* WhatsApp Business Account information
* Meta Business Account ID
* Phone Number ID
* WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID

### Messaging Data

To provide WhatsApp messaging services, we may process:

* Customer phone numbers
* Message templates
* Campaign details
* Message delivery status
* Message timestamps
* Message identifiers

### Usage Information

We automatically collect certain technical information to help operate, secure, and improve our services, including:

* IP address
* Browser type and version
* Device type
* Operating system
* Pages visited within the platform
* Session activity (such as login times and feature usage)
* Error logs and diagnostic information

### Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

* Keep you signed in
* Remember your preferences
* Improve website performance
* Analyze usage and platform performance

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

* Provide and maintain our services.
* Authenticate users.

* Deliver transactional and promotional messages that you initiate.
* Improve platform performance and user experience.
* Monitor security, detect fraud, and prevent abuse.
* Respond to customer support requests.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

If you connect your Meta or WhatsApp Business account through Engage:
